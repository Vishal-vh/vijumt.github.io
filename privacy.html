Privacy Policy

Aakhri update: November 2026

Ye policy batati hai ki Viju MT website aur Android app aapki jaankari ke saath kya karte hain. Website ya app use karke aap isse sahmat hote hain.

1. Hum kya jaankari lete hain

Hum aapse naam, phone number ya email jaisi koi personal jaankari nahi maangte, aur account banana zaruri nahi hai. Kuch technical jaankari apne aap ban sakti hai, jaise device ka type, Android version, IP address aur app ka use.

2. Ads aur third-party services

Viju MT free hai aur ads se chalta hai. Website aur app me banner aur video ads dikhte hain, jo third-party ad networks (jaise Google IMA SDK aur doosre ad partners) dete hain. Ye networks ads dikhane aur unki performance naapne ke liye cookies, advertising ID ya milte-julte tools use kar sakte hain. Hum in networks ke data ko control nahi karte. Unki apni privacy policy lagu hoti hai.

Aap apne phone ki Settings me jakar Advertising ID reset ya "Ads personalization" band kar sakte hain.

3. Cookies aur local storage

Website khud koi cookie save nahi karti. Par ad partners cookies laga sakte hain. Browser ki settings se aap cookies band ya delete kar sakte hain.

4. Permissions (Android app)

  • Internet: video, channels aur content chalane ke liye.
  • Storage / Downloads: jab aap kuch download karte hain.
  • Notifications: nayi update ya alerts ke liye.

Hum in permissions se aapka personal data nahi bechte.

5. Content

Viju MT khud koi movie ya channel host nahi karta. Content ke link bahar ke sources se aate hain. Sabhi trademarks aur content unke maalikon ke hain. Kisi content par aapatti ho to neeche diye email par bataiye.

6. Bachchon ki privacy

Ye service 13 saal se kam umar ke bachchon ke liye nahi hai. Hum jaankar kisi bachche ka data nahi lete.

7. Data ki suraksha

Hum jaankari ko surakshit rakhne ki koshish karte hain, par internet par koi tarika 100% surakshit nahi hota.

8. Policy me badlav

Ye policy kabhi bhi badal sakti hai. Naya version isi page par upar di gayi date ke saath dikhega.
